    Good to know

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I try any plan before choosing a paid subscription?

    Yes. Every plan includes a free 14-day trial, with no card required to start.

    Can different people at my school use Clasten?

    Clasten supports separate administrator, employee and parent experiences. Access depends on each account's role and the school's plan.

    How many students can each plan support?

    Student limits depend on the plan. Check the plan cards above for each tier's current limit and included features.

    Can I manage more than one campus?

    Multiple campuses are available on Enterprise. A school can use one subscription and Paystack account across its campuses, with the same fee types and amounts at every campus.

    Which billing periods can I choose?

    You can pay per term or annually. The Standard and Enterprise annual discount is available once, on a school's first annual subscription purchase; annual renewals are billed at the full annual price.

    Can parents pay school fees online?

    Online fee invoices and Paystack payments are available on Standard and Enterprise. The school connects its own Paystack account, and payments go to that account.

    What happens when my free trial ends?

    A school administrator can select a paid plan from the subscription settings. If the trial expires first, record changes pause until the subscription is activated.

    How do teachers manage their timetable?

    Teachers can add lessons for their assigned class and subjects, view their timetable on the dashboard, and receive schedule updates in their school inbox.

    How are school fees displayed?

    School fee records use naira (NGN). Available parent payment features depend on the school's subscription plan.

    How can I get help?

    Email jayomoluwa@gmail.com and include your school name and a description of what you need.

    Refund and Cancellation Policy

    Last updated: 16 November 2026   Version: 1.0

    This policy applies to schools using Clasten's school-management service. Plan availability, features, prices, and billing periods are shown before payment.

    1. Subscription plans and pricing

    Clasten offers the plans and billing periods displayed during registration and checkout, including per-term and annual billing where available. The price shown for the selected plan and billing period is the amount due. Prices may change, but any updated price will be shown before a new payment is completed.

    Eligible new schools may start a 14-day free trial without paying a subscription fee. If a school chooses to continue with a paid plan, payment is processed through Paystack. By completing payment, the school authorizes the transaction for the plan and billing period it selected.

    2. Free trial and paid subscription

    The 14-day free trial is available to eligible new schools and does not automatically create a paid subscription. When the trial ends, a school must select and pay for a plan to continue using subscription-protected features. No refund is due for the free-trial period because no subscription fee is charged for that period.

    3. Subscription billing and renewal

    Subscriptions are paid in advance as one-time transactions for the billing period selected at checkout and do not automatically renew. No further subscription charge is made unless the school initiates another payment. A school may choose not to purchase a new subscription period at any time; this does not immediately end access already paid for, and access continues until the current subscription expiry date. Choosing not to renew does not by itself qualify for a refund.

    4. Seven-day refund window

    A school may request a refund within seven (7) calendar days of a successful subscription payment if it purchased the subscription in error, has not substantially used the platform or premium features, and submits its request within the seven-day window. Each request is reviewed, including relevant account activity, and may be approved or declined based on these criteria and applicable law. Requests submitted after seven calendar days may not be eligible for a refund.

    5. Duplicate or accidental charges

    If a school is charged more than once for the same subscription transaction because of a payment-processing error, Clasten will refund a verified duplicate charge. The school may be asked to provide the Paystack transaction reference, payment date, and other information needed to verify the charge.

    6. Subscription cancellation

    • Subscriptions do not auto-renew, so a school does not need to cancel to avoid a future charge.
    • A school may choose not to make another subscription payment at any time.
    • Not renewing does not immediately end access already paid for; paid features remain available until the current subscription expiry date, subject to the Terms of Service.
    • Not renewing does not automatically qualify the school for a refund.

    7. Subscription upgrades

    When a school selects a higher-tier plan, the price and billing period for the new subscription are shown before payment. Higher-tier features become available after payment is successfully processed and the subscription is activated. Payments for an activated upgrade are generally non-refundable, except for eligible requests under the seven-day refund window, verified duplicate or erroneous charges, or where a refund is required by law.

    8. Subscription downgrades

    A school may select a lower-tier plan for a new subscription. The school should review the plan features and limits shown before confirming payment, as a lower tier may not include features or capacity available on a higher tier. No refund, credit, or prorated adjustment is provided for an unused portion of a previously paid higher-tier subscription, except where required by law.

    9. SMS and notification features

    Messaging and notification features included with a subscription are subject to the plan's stated features and limits, third-party delivery availability, and service reliability. Clasten does not sell separate SMS-credit bundles as part of these subscriptions. Unused access to included notification features is not refundable as a separate cash item.

    10. Service interruptions

    If a significant service disruption or technical issue materially affects availability, Clasten may, at its discretion, extend the affected subscription period or provide equivalent service credit. A subscription extension is the preferred remedy for a verified service disruption.

    11. Non-refundable circumstances

    Except where a refund is approved under this policy or required by law, refunds will generally not be granted for:

    • A change of mind after the seven-day refund window.
    • Failure to use a subscription.
    • Service that has already been consumed or substantially used.
    • A violation of the Terms of Service.
    • Fraud, abuse, unlawful activity, or misuse of the platform.
    • Account suspension or termination caused by the school's breach of Clasten's policies.

    12. Account suspension or termination

    Where a school account is suspended or terminated because of a breach of the Terms of Service, fraud, abuse, unauthorized access attempts, false information, or activity that compromises platform integrity or security, Clasten is not obligated to issue a refund, except where required by applicable law.

    13. Refund processing time

    Approved refunds are generally initiated within three (3) to five (5) business days. The time for funds to appear may vary depending on Paystack, the school's bank, or the payment method used.

The parties should first attempt to resolve the matter through customer support, billing review, and management escalation within a reasonable period.     The information handled depends on the school and the features it uses. It may include:

    • Student records: names, student identifiers, class and academic details, attendance, results, admissions information, guardian links, and other information entered by the school.
    • Parent and guardian details: names, contact information, relationships to students, and communications.
    • Employee information: names, work contact details, roles, departments, class assignments, timetables, and access permissions.

    • Communications and technical information: messages, announcements, and support requests. In-app feedback may include the user's school, role, current portal section, message, and optional reply email. We do not collect screenshots or session credentials through that form. Limited browser storage values and technical or security events may also be used to operate the service.
